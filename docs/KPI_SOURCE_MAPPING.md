# KPI Source Mapping Included in Seed Data

The seed data includes the earlier KPI templates plus the newly supplied role material.

## Newly added templates

### SVP Projects – Government Projects
Source: `SVP KPI.pdf`

Includes Government Project Management, SLA Adherence, Billing & Invoicing, Collections / Receivables, Penalty / LD Control, Client / Government Coordination, Project Profitability & Cost Control, Compliance & Documentation, and Team Performance. Numeric targets explicitly available in the source are retained (100% SLA, 100% invoice timeliness, >=95% collections, NIL penalty/LD). The source does not give KRA weightages, so the seed uses clearly tagged recommended system weights that HR can version and change.

### AVP Technology – Java Full Stack & Government Projects

### Team Lead – Java Full Stack

### Business Analyst & Testing Lead
Source: `Business Analyst & Testing Lead.pdf`

Includes all listed KRAs/frequencies: Client Requirement Gathering, Requirement Analysis & Mapping, Mock-up Preparation, Requirement Documentation, Test Case Preparation, Automation & Load Testing, Knowledge Transfer, Functional & Scenario Testing, Bug Identification & Reporting, QC Documentation & Status Tracking, Client Demo & Delivery Mapping, Scope Creep Identification, Customer & Developer Coordination, QA Team Supervision, and Management Reporting. The source does not specify weightages, so the seed uses tagged recommended system weights totaling 100.

### Sales – Tender, Business Development & Collections
Source: `Sales KRA KPI(New) (1).csv`

Includes all seven source KRAs and their bullet KPI areas: Revenue & Conversions, Tender Management, Business Development, Client Management, Collections, Compliance & Reporting, and Market Intelligence & Strategy. The supplied CSV has no populated Measurement, Target or Frequency values and no weightage column. The seed therefore uses objective-choice scoring plus tagged recommended weights; HR should set company-specific numeric targets before production use where appropriate.
